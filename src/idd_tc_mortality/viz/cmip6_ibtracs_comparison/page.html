Scorecard

All kept places at the selected level for the selected outcome, model series, statistic, window and start year. Click a column header to sort. ρ is given raw and detrended side by side; the remaining measures follow the Trend toggle. The chance band is the 2.5–97.5% range of ρ against phase-randomized surrogates of the observed series (bold ρ = outside it); the ceiling is the median (min–max) run-vs-run ρ at the same window; neff is the Bretherton et al. (1999) effective sample size.

Deaths, three ways

Observed deaths (v1985 file, 1985–2023) against the shipped gpd_cens e7a78b19 model predicted on the IBTrACS storm record (band = the 100 fit draws: mortality-model uncertainty alone) and on the CMIP6 storm record (band = 800 run × draw values: hazard uncertainty with fit draws averaged out). Years before 2000 are out of sample for the v2000 fit (shaded). The rake is one ratio per super-region, so within a super-region the rank measures are identical for adjusted and unadjusted deaths.
